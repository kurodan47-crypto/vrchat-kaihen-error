What you see on screen:

Material Slots: … (SDK performance stats / rank breakdown)

What it means

Every material slot is a separate draw call. PC ranks: Excellent 4, Good 8, Medium 16, Poor 32. Quest/mobile: Excellent 1, Good 1, Medium 2, Poor 4 - go over and your rank drops. Quest shows only Medium or better by default, so with 3 or more material slots most Quest users won't see your avatar. Slots on outfits and props that are turned off still count.

Common causes

  • Every outfit and prop is kept on the avatar, just turned off
  • The body, hair, outfit and accessories each use their own material
  • A renderer has more materials than its mesh has sub-meshes (the extra slots just draw the last part again - pure waste)
  • The Quest version still uses the PC version's materials

How to fix it

  1. Open the SDK Builder panel, expand the rank breakdown and check Material Slots
  2. If a Mesh Renderer / Skinned Mesh Renderer has more Materials than the mesh has sub-meshes, remove the extra slots (nothing changes visually)
  3. Delete outfits and props you never wear, or move them to a separate copy you don't upload
  4. Merge parts that share the same material with AAO (Avatar Optimizer) Merge Skinned Mesh or a similar tool
  5. Combine textures into one atlas (with an atlas tool or Blender) so several parts can share one material
  6. Make the Quest version on a duplicate and aim for 2 material slots or fewer (Medium). If an outfit comes with a single-material version, use it

Before you change anything, make a full copy of your project folder so you can go back.
