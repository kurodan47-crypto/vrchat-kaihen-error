Outfit not following the body in VRChat (bone name mismatch) - fix

What you see on screen:

(No error message - the outfit doesn't move with the body)

What it means

If the outfit and avatar name their bones differently, the outfit won't follow the body.

Common causes

  • Naming differences like UpperArm.L vs Left_UpperArm

How to fix it

  1. Rename the outfit's bones to match the avatar
  2. Use Modular Avatar's "Setup Outfit"

Before you change anything, make a full copy of your project folder so you can go back.
