What you see on screen:

Missing Credentials

What it means

Your VRChat login isn't being passed to the SDK correctly.

Common causes

  • A VPN or Cloudflare WARP is running
  • Your login session expired

How to fix it

  1. Turn off VPN / WARP
  2. Log out and log back in from the SDK panel
  3. Upload again

Before you change anything, make a full copy of your project folder so you can go back.
