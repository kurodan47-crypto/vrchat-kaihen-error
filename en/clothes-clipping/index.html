What you see on screen:

(No error message - skin shows through the outfit at the chest, belly, elbows or knees)

What it means

The body mesh is sticking out past the outfit mesh on top of it. Unity gives no error for this, so you normally have to hunt for the spots by eye. Fixing it in the standing pose isn't always enough - it can come back when you crouch or raise your arms.

Common causes

  • The outfit wasn't made for this avatar (a non-supported outfit)
  • You changed the avatar's body shape keys (chest size, weight, etc.) but not the outfit's
  • The outfit's bones don't line up with the body's, or the outfit was rescaled
  • The outfit sits so close to the skin that the two flicker at a distance (z-fighting)

How to fix it

  1. If you changed body shape keys, set the outfit's shape keys with the same names to the same values (Modular Avatar's Blendshape Sync keeps them matched)
  2. If the outfit or avatar comes with "shrink / hide body under clothes" shape keys, use them (check the readme)
  3. Remove the body parts that the outfit covers at upload time with AAO (Avatar Optimizer) Remove Mesh By Mask or a similar component - the original mesh is not changed
  4. Re-attach the outfit's bones to the body with Modular Avatar's Setup Outfit
  5. Enter Play mode or change poses and check with arms raised and while crouching
  6. If some spots still show, push the outfit outward a little in Blender

Before you change anything, make a full copy of your project folder so you can go back.
