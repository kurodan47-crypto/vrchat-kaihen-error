VRChat avatar over the 256-bit synced parameter limit - how to fix

What you see on screen:

Too many synced parameters / exceeds the maximum (256 bits)

What it means

The total of your synced parameters is over the 256-bit limit. This happens when you add a lot of gimmicks.

Common causes

  • Many gimmicks and outfit toggles
  • Parameters added by Modular Avatar count toward the total too

How to fix it

  1. Remove gimmicks you don't use
  2. Turn off syncing for parameters that don't need it (bool = 1 bit, int/float = 8 bits)

Before you change anything, make a full copy of your project folder so you can go back.
