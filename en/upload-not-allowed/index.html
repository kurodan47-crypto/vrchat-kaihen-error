VRChat "You do not have permission to upload" - account can't upload yet

What you see on screen:

You do not have permission to upload / uploading is disabled

What it means

Your VRChat account hasn't reached the level where uploading is allowed.

Common causes

  • Your account is new and its Trust Rank is too low

How to fix it

  1. Play VRChat for a while and upload once you reach "New User" or higher

Before you change anything, make a full copy of your project folder so you can go back.
