What you see on screen:

Avatar Failed Upload / Failed to upload

What it means

The upload failed partway through. There are several possible causes.

Common causes

  • A duplicate Blueprint ID
  • Another red error appeared first
  • Network connection or VPN

How to fix it

  1. Press "Detach" on the Pipeline Manager, restart Unity, and try again
  2. Fix the first red error in the Console
  3. Turn off any VPN or Cloudflare WARP

Before you change anything, make a full copy of your project folder so you can go back.
