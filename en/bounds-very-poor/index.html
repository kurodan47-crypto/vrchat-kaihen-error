What you see on screen:

Bounds Size: … (SDK performance stats / rank breakdown)

What it means

Bounds Size is the box that holds every renderer on the avatar. PC and Quest use the same limits: Excellent up to 2.5 x 2.5 x 2.5 m, Good 4 x 4 x 4 m, Medium and Poor 5 x 6 x 5 m - anything bigger is Very Poor. One mesh or particle system with a huge or far-away box is enough to make the whole avatar Very Poor, and the SDK shows only the total, not which object causes it. Bounds Size alone never gets your avatar hidden by other people's performance settings, but it does lower the overall rank they see.

Common causes

  • A Skinned Mesh Renderer has oversized Bounds (some outfits ship with a very large box so they never disappear)
  • Something sits far from the body: a world-fixed gimmick, a prop parked metres away, a leftover object
  • Particle systems that spread wide (Trail and Line Renderers do not count toward Bounds)
  • The avatar itself is big (large wings, tail or props)
  • Scale was changed on the avatar or an outfit, and the boxes grew with it

How to fix it

  1. Open the SDK Builder panel, expand the rank breakdown and read Bounds Size
  2. Select each Skinned Mesh Renderer and look at Bounds (Center / Extent). Extent is half the size - an Extent of 3 means a 6 m box
  3. Press Edit Bounds on the Skinned Mesh Renderer and shrink the box until it just holds the mesh with the arms raised
  4. Look in the Hierarchy for objects far from the body, and move them closer or remove them
  5. For outfits dressed with Modular Avatar, set the box in MA Mesh Settings - its values win at upload
  6. Build again and check Bounds Size

Before you change anything, make a full copy of your project folder so you can go back.
