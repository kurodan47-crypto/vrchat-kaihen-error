VRChat audio clip without "Load In Background" enabled - upload fix

What you see on screen:

Found an audio clip with load type `Decompress On Load` which doesn't have `Load In Background` enabled.

What it means

An audio clip with load type Decompress On Load has "Load In Background" turned off. You can't upload with this setting.

Common causes

  • You added a gimmick with sound effects or voice
  • You added your own audio files

How to fix it

  1. Select the audio file (.wav, .mp3, etc.) in the Project window
  2. Check "Load In Background" in the Inspector
  3. Press Apply and upload again

Before you change anything, make a full copy of your project folder so you can go back.
