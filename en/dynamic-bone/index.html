Dynamic Bone left on a VRChat avatar - convert to PhysBones

What you see on screen:

DynamicBone … could not be found

What it means

Dynamic Bone is no longer used in VRChat. It has been replaced by PhysBones.

Common causes

  • It was left over on an older avatar or outfit

How to fix it

  1. Convert it to PhysBones, e.g. with the VRChat SDK's conversion feature
  2. Remove any you don't need

Before you change anything, make a full copy of your project folder so you can go back.
