"Attempting to upload an avatar we do not own" in VRChat - how to fix

What you see on screen:

Attempting to upload an avatar we do not own / clearing blueprint

What it means

The upload destination ID (Blueprint ID) points to an avatar that isn't yours.

Common causes

  • The purchased avatar prefab still has the creator's Blueprint ID
  • You reused an avatar uploaded from another account

How to fix it

  1. Select the avatar and press "Detach" on the Pipeline Manager
  2. Log out in the SDK panel and restart Unity
  3. Log in again and upload

Before you change anything, make a full copy of your project folder so you can go back.
