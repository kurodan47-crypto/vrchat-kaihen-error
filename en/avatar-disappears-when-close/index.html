What you see on screen:

(No error. The whole avatar, or its clothes, hair or props, vanish when you get close or look from some angles)

What it means

People mean two different things by this. If other people's whole avatars vanish when they come near you, that is VRChat's Personal Space setting - the avatar is not broken. If only the clothes, hair or a prop of one avatar vanish, that mesh's Bounds are wrong. Unity stops drawing a mesh when its Bounds box is outside the camera view, so the mesh disappears even though it should still be in view.

Common causes

  • Personal Space is on in VRChat (Settings > Comfort & Safety): it hides other users' avatars when they come very close (friends are not affected)
  • A Skinned Mesh Renderer's Bounds are too small or in the wrong place (common after dressing an outfit made for another avatar, or changing Root Bone or scale)
  • Each outfit part has its own Root Bone and Bounds, so one part is culled before the others
  • In your own first-person view, anything under the Head bone is hidden on purpose (you still see it in a mirror) - that is normal
  • Anything very close to the camera is cut off by the near clipping distance

How to fix it

  1. If other people's whole avatars vanish: open the VRChat menu > Settings > Comfort & Safety and turn Personal Space off
  2. If only clothes or hair vanish: select that mesh in Unity and check that it sits inside the Bounds box of its Skinned Mesh Renderer (drawn as a wire box in the Scene when selected)
  3. Set Root Bone to Hips on every Skinned Mesh Renderer near the body and give them the same Bounds - big enough to hold the whole avatar with the arms raised
  4. With Modular Avatar, add MA Mesh Settings to the avatar root and set Bounds and Root Bone there (outfits added with Setup Outfit follow it)
  5. Do not make the box huge: Bounds Size counts toward the performance rank (over 5 x 6 x 5 m is Very Poor)
  6. Upload and check in front of a mirror and from another person's view, up close and from below

Before you change anything, make a full copy of your project folder so you can go back.
